What it does

Three engines, each answering a question no team can answer by hand.

Engine 01 · Call intelligence

Every call transcript, read and tagged

AI-scored sentiment, intent, entities and a summary for each call. Searchable call log with the full transcript, intent breakdown, top complaint themes, and performance by delivery area and by merchant.

Engine 02 · Support conversations

Chats and tickets, classified and SLA-tracked

Sentiment, intent and the exact negative trigger on every conversation, enriched with merchant and zone. Sentiment trend, top triggers, channel comparison, geographic heatmap, live SLA-breach list, flag and resolve, CSV export.

Engine 03 · Cancellation risk

Orders scored before you lose them

A risk score per live order with the driver named — merchant, zone, hour, day, basket size or who cancelled. Plus a written drivers report and an AI chat that answers questions against the underlying data.

Why it matters

Read every conversation, not a sample of them.

Most QA teams score a handful of tickets a week and call it a trend. Clarity scores all of them, every day, and shows what actually moved.

  • Find it the same day. A rising complaint driver appears as a trend line and an alert while it is still small — not in next month's report.
  • Fix the right thing. Every number drills through to the merchant, zone, hour and the customer's own words behind it.
  • Agree on one score. A composite health score built from sentiment, cancellations and escalations that agents, managers and execs all read the same way.
